Pixel Pause Support

Help with Pixel Pause, the calm colour-by-number game for iPhone and iPad.

Write to us: sanjaykeyan.developer@gmail.com. We usually answer within two working days. In the app, Settings > Contact support starts the same email.

I bought Lifetime, but books still show as locked.

Open Settings > Restore purchases while signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Lifetime is also shared with your Family Sharing group; family members use Restore purchases in the same way.

My paintings are not on my other device.

Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud turned on for Pixel Pause (iOS Settings, then your name, then iCloud). Settings in the app shows whether syncing is working. Paintings sync when the app is opened or brought back to the front.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. A photo is turned into a painting on your device and is never uploaded. Pixel Pause keeps only the painting, not the photo.

How do I paint a long stroke?

Choose a colour, then put a finger on a cell of that colour and drag: the cells of that colour under your finger paint as you go. A drag that starts anywhere else (on a cell that is already painted, on another colour or on the empty canvas) moves the picture instead, and two fingers move and zoom it. Settings > How to paint replays the guided first painting.

I can't find the last few cells.

Tap Find to jump to a cell that is still missing in the chosen colour. Zoomed out, the cells still to paint in that colour are marked.

How do I save or share a finished painting?

When you finish, tap Share. Choose Save Image to keep it in Photos, or any app to send it.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

In the app's Settings. If iOS notifications for Pixel Pause are off, turn them on in the iOS Settings app first.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings > Erase local data. If iCloud is on for Pixel Pause, the paintings kept in iCloud come back the next time you open the app. To remove the iCloud copy too, open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, choose Pixel Pause and delete its data.
