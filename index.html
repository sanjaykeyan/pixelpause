Privacy Policy

Pixel Pause · Last updated 2 September 2026

Pixel Pause is a colour-by-number painting app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what it does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app has no accounts, collects nothing about you directly, and your photos never leave your device.

No account, no sign-up

Pixel Pause does not ask for a name, email address, phone number or password. There is nothing to register and no profile. We do not build a profile of you and we do not sell or share personal information.

Your photos stay on your device

Pixel Pause can turn a photo from your library into a painting. When you choose a photo:

  • The conversion happens entirely on your device. The image is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The original photo is not stored by the app. Only the resulting grid of colour numbers, its palette, and a small thumbnail are kept.
  • Access to your photo library is requested only at the moment you tap to choose a photo, and only for that purpose.

Your progress and iCloud

Painting progress, the paintings you create, and your daily helper count are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync through Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud database, so your work follows you between your devices.

That private database belongs to your Apple account. We cannot read it, and neither can any third party. Apple's handling of it is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. Turning off iCloud Drive for Pixel Pause in iOS Settings stops this syncing; the app continues to work entirely offline.

Purchases

Pixel Pause Lifetime is a single, one-time purchase — there is no subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details, billing address or Apple ID. The app only learns whether the purchase exists, so it knows what to unlock.

Advertising

The free version of Pixel Pause can show an advert after you finish a painting. These are served by Google AdMob. Buying Lifetime removes them permanently.

  • Pixel Pause requests non-personalised adverts only. They are not targeted using a profile of your interests or behaviour.
  • The app does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not track you across other apps or websites. It declares no tracking in its App Store privacy information.
  • To serve and measure any advert, Google still receives technical data such as your IP address, device type and operating system version. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Where required, the app shows a consent form before requesting adverts. You can reopen it at any time from Settings → Ad choices inside the app.

What we do not use

Pixel Pause contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social-network SDKs. There is no Firebase Analytics, no Sentry, no advertising attribution framework, and no third-party tracker beyond the AdMob advertising described above.

Children

Pixel Pause is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because there are no accounts and no personal data collection, there is no profile for us to delete. If you are a parent and have a question, please get in touch.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Erase local data inside the app removes all painting progress and every painting you have created from the device.
  • To remove the synced copy, turn off iCloud for Pixel Pause, or delete the app's iCloud data from iOS Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in Pixel Pause, can be sent through the support contact on the Pixel Pause App Store listing.
