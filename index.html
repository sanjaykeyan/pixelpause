Pixel Pause Privacy Policy

Pixel Pause · Last updated 10 November 2026

Pixel Pause is a colour-by-number game for iPhone, published on the App Store by Sanjay Keyan ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

In short: Pixel Pause has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking across other companies' apps. The free version shows a few adverts from Google, never personalised; Lifetime shows none. Your photos never leave your device. Your progress syncs only through your own private iCloud, which we cannot read. We receive no personal information about you and sell none; what Google's advertising software receives is described under Advertising.

No account, no sign-up

You never create an account or give us a name, email address or password. We keep no profile of you.

Your photos stay on your device

When you turn a photo into a painting, you choose it in the system photo picker, which gives Pixel Pause only the photo you pick. The photo is converted into a painting on your device and is never uploaded. Pixel Pause keeps only the painting it makes (a grid of numbered cells, its colours and a small preview), not the photo.

If you save a finished painting to your photo library, iOS asks first for permission to add photos. Pixel Pause can only add images; it cannot see your library through that permission. Sharing a painting hands the image to the iOS share sheet, and it goes only where you send it.

Your progress and iCloud

Your painting progress and your photo paintings are stored on your device, as are your settings. If you are signed in to iCloud, the progress and paintings also sync through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database, so your other devices can see them. That database belongs to your iCloud account: we cannot read it, and the synced data is stored encrypted. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Pixel Pause in the iOS Settings app; everything keeps working on the device.

Purchases

Pixel Pause Lifetime is a single purchase, with no subscription. Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We never receive your card details, billing address or Apple Account details; the app only learns from Apple whether Lifetime is unlocked. If you share purchases with Family Sharing, Apple tells the app the same for each family member.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device and shown by iOS. Pixel Pause does not use push notifications, and nothing about the reminder is sent to us. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in the iOS Settings app.

App updates

Pixel Pause can receive small updates (fixes and new pictures) without a trip to the App Store, through Expo's update service. When the app checks for an update, it sends what is needed to choose the right one: the app's version, your device's operating system, the identifier of the update the app is running, and a random token, created when the app was installed, that tells Expo whether this installation has checked before. If the app stopped because of an error in the first seconds after it started, the next check also sends a short description of that error, up to about 1,000 characters. None of this identifies you: the token and the identifiers are not linked to you, your Apple Account or any hardware identifier, and all of it is used only to deliver updates.

Advertising

The free version of Pixel Pause shows adverts served by Google AdMob. With Lifetime there are none, from the moment it is unlocked.

  • When you leave a finished painting, a full-screen advert may appear: at most one every ten minutes, never after the guided first painting, and not before you have finished three paintings.
  • When you run out of Find or Bucket, you may choose to watch a short video advert for one more. It is always your choice, the reward is stated before the video starts, closing it early earns nothing, and it is offered at most five times a day. The helper it earns is kept on your device and is not sent to us.

Your choice. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent form appears before any advert, and you can change your answer at any time in the app's Settings under Ad choices. To report an advert that seems wrong, use Report an ad in the same place.

Never personalised. Pixel Pause asks Google for non-personalised adverts only, rated for general audiences (G). It never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so Google's software cannot read your device's advertising identifier.

What Google's software collects. To show adverts, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the app sends Google information about your device and its use of adverts: identifiers for the app installation and the advertising request (not the advertising identifier), your IP address, which Google uses to estimate your general location, which adverts were shown and tapped, and performance, crash and diagnostic information about the advertising software. Google uses it under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), and explains how it uses information from apps that show its adverts at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We see only totals in AdMob, such as how many adverts were shown, never information about you.

What we do not use

Apart from Google's advertising software described above, Pixel Pause contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social-media software, and does not track you across apps or websites. The only error text the app sends on its own is the one the update check can send, described under App updates; an error in a support email goes only if you send that email (see Contact). If you choose to share crash reports with developers in the iOS Settings app, Apple passes them on without identifying you.

Children

Pixel Pause is suitable for everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, and the app asks Google only for adverts rated for general audiences (G).

Deleting your data

  • On this device: Settings in the app, then Erase local data. Deleting the app removes everything on the device too. If iCloud is on for Pixel Pause, what is saved in iCloud comes back the next time you open the app, so delete the iCloud copy as well to remove it for good.
  • In iCloud: in the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), choose Pixel Pause and delete its data.

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to delete or export on our side. For what Google's advertising software has received, see Google's privacy policy linked under Advertising. If you have a question about your data or your rights, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date. A change that affects what information leaves your device will be made before the version of the app that needs it is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Pixel Pause: sanjaykeyan.developer@gmail.com. You can also use Contact support in the app's Settings. It opens a draft email to that address with some details below your own words: the app's version and build, the update identifier, your iOS version and device model, whether Lifetime is unlocked, the iCloud sync status shown in Settings, the content version and, if there is one, the last error the app noted on your device (a short message and the screen it happened on). You can read and delete any of it before you send; the app sends nothing itself, and the details never include your name, your Apple Account, your photos or your paintings.
